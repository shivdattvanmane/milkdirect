1. Executive Summary & Project Vision

MilkDirect is a purpose-built hyperlocal dairy marketplace engineered to disintermediate the conventional dairy distribution chain. In traditional supply chains, milk travels through multiple middlemen, chilling centers, and industrial processing plants, taking 48 to 72 hours to reach urban households. This industrial cycle strips the milk of its natural flavor, promotes adulteration risks, and severely reduces the profit margin of grassroot dairy farmers.

⚠️ The Conventional Dairy Crisis
  • Delayed Delivery: Packaged pouch milk is often 2–3 days old before reaching doorsteps.
  • Middleman Margins: Aggregators and brands extract up to 45% of consumer retail value.
  • Opaque Nutrition: Fat & SNF levels are standardized artificially by blending powdered solids.
  • Severe Adulteration: Chemical preservation agents and synthetic stabilizers are common.
🥛 The MilkDirect Paradigm
  • Sub-4-Hour Milking-to-Door: Morning milking delivered by 7:00 AM; Evening by 7:00 PM.
  • 100% Farmer Margin: Direct UPI payouts from consumer to farmer with zero commission cut.
  • Complete Transparency: Lab-tested Fat% & SNF% declared per cattle listing.
  • Trust & Accountability: Hyperlocal customer review aggregate and admin FSSAI audit.
Key Strategic Differentiator: Unlike grocery quick-commerce platforms that operate expensive dark stores and high-inventory warehouses, MilkDirect operates as an asset-light, decentralized marketplace. The platform manages ordering logic, subscriptions, verification, and route schedules, while physical delivery is executed directly by local farmers and village milkmen.

MilkDirect — Project Documentation Section 3: Authentication & Multi-Role Gateway

3. Multi-Role Identity & Gateway System

Security and role segregation are foundational to MilkDirect. Users interact with the platform through three distinct personas: Customer, Supplier (Farmer / Milkman), and Administrator.

Customer
Explores nearby dairy farms, manages delivery addresses, customizes recurring daily milk subscriptions, tracks deliveries, and pays suppliers directly via UPI.
Supplier
Configures cattle listings (Cow/Buffalo), pricing, fat/SNF stats, delivery PIN codes, and executes daily dispatch routes with 1-tap delivery confirmations.
Administrator
Verifies supplier KYC and FSSAI credentials, moderates user accounts, arbitrates dispute tickets, and monitors platform-wide business analytics.

Strict Role-Enforcement & Security Controls

Cross-Role Collision Prevention: A registered Supplier cannot log in through the Customer portal, and a registered Customer cannot log in through the Farmer/Milkman portal. If a user attempts a mismatched role login, the client instantly executes a clean sign-out and notifies the user with role-specific guidance:
"This email is registered as a Supplier. Please use the Farmer / Milkman Login."
  • Role Persistence & Instant Switching: The app stores the chosen portal in SharedPreferences (last_role). On subsequent launches, it bypasses the role selection screen and loads the relevant login immediately. Tapping "Change Role" instantly flushes the local preference and returns to the master entry gate.
  • Live 3-Segment Password Strength Meter: Built into all registration forms, providing real-time visual feedback: Weak (<8 chars or numeric only) → Fair (Mixed chars) → Strong (8+ chars alphanumeric).
  • Zero In-App Admin Registration: To prevent unauthorized escalation, admin registration forms are completely absent from the client. Administrators are provisioned exclusively by direct database administrator intervention in the Supabase console.
  • Account Suspension (is_blocked): If any user account is flagged for fraud or malicious behavior, administrators can toggle is_blocked = true, immediately aborting their current session and barring future logins.
MilkDirect — Project Documentation Section 4: Deep-Dive Portal Specifications

4. Deep-Dive Portal Specifications

4.1 Customer Experience Portal

Designed for frictionless discovery, automated morning delivery, and complete visibility into milk provenance:

🔍 Hyperlocal Supplier Discovery
  • PIN Code Filtering: Only shows suppliers serving the customer's active delivery PIN code.
  • Cattle & Milk Type Filtering: 1-tap filters for Pure Cow Milk, Buffalo Milk, or Mixed.
  • Processing Transparency: Labels Raw (unboiled), Boiled, or Pasteurised milk.
  • Quality Badges: Displays certified Fat %, SNF %, distance, and average review stars.
📅 Recurring Subscription Engine
  • Custom Delivery Schedules: Daily, Alternate Days, or Custom Weekdays (e.g. Mon-Wed-Fri).
  • Configurable Quantity: 0.5L, 1.0L, 1.5L, 2.0L+ per delivery slot.
  • Delivery Slot Choice: Morning (5:00–8:00 AM) or Evening (5:00–8:00 PM).
  • Pause & Resume: Vacation mode allows 1-tap suspension of deliveries without canceling.
📍 Smart Multi-Address Book
  • Multiple Delivery Points: Store Home, Office, or Parents' addresses.
  • Precision Coordinates: Captures latitude/longitude for optimal delivery routing.
  • Detailed Landmarks: Flat/House number, street, society, and local landmark fields.
💳 Billing & Dispute Ticketing
  • Direct UPI Payments: Monthly ledger with direct link to supplier's UPI VPA.
  • Delivery Audit Calendar: Interactive calendar showing delivered vs missed days.
  • Complaint Desk: Raise dispute tickets for sour milk, late arrival, or quantity issues.

4.2 Supplier (Farmer & Milkman) Portal

Engineered to empower rural cattle owners with digital route management, listing autonomy, and immediate cash flow:

📋 4-Step Registration Wizard
  • Step 1 (Identity): Classification as Farmer (Own Cattle) vs Milkman (Village Sourced), farm name, and contact details.
  • Step 2 (Service Area): Farm village, home PIN code, and multi-PIN service delivery coverage list.
  • Step 3 (Payouts & Slots): Direct UPI ID, active delivery slots, minimum order quantity, and delivery fee.
  • Step 4 (Quality & Legal): Optional FSSAI license upload, hygiene declaration, and purity commitment.
🚚 Daily Dispatch & Execution Sheet
  • Route Organization: Morning and Evening delivery runs sorted by address sequence.
  • 1-Tap Status Logging: Quick action buttons to mark each bottle as Delivered or Missed.
  • Direct Customer Dialing: Integrated call and WhatsApp buttons to resolve delivery issues.
  • Realtime Yield Metrics: Daily litres required calculation for morning milking scheduling.

4.3 Administrator Oversight Portal

Provides complete administrative governance, verification vetting, and community dispute arbitration:

🛡️ Supplier KYC & FSSAI Verification
Pending supplier profiles are held in an unverified state (hidden from consumer search) until the admin reviews their farm credentials, FSSAI numbers, and declarations. Admin can Approve or Reject with a custom explanation.
⚖️ Dispute Arbitration & User Safety
Centralized inbox of customer complaints. Administrators inspect order history, contact both parties, issue resolutions, and have the power to instantly suspend or block fraudulent profiles (is_blocked).

5. Database Architecture & Data Schema

MilkDirect runs on PostgreSQL inside Supabase. All tables, functions, triggers, and foreign keys utilize the mk_ namespace prefix. This allows MilkDirect to coexist harmoniously in shared Supabase environments alongside other projects (such as queuekissan) without naming collisions.

Row Level Security (RLS) & Multi-Tenant Isolation

Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) is strictly enabled across all mk_ tables. Customers can only read and write their own addresses, subscriptions, and complaints. Unverified suppliers are hidden from public product queries via verification_status = 'verified' filters. Only users with mk_profiles.role = 'admin' are granted global update permissions.

8. Deployment, Setup & Administration Guide

Step 1: Supabase Configuration

  1. Enable Email Provider in Supabase Dashboard → Authentication → Providers → Email.
  2. Configure password policy to enforce a minimum of 8 characters.
  3. Execute supabase/schema.sql in the Supabase SQL editor to create all mk_ tables and triggers.

Step 2: Provisioning the First Master Admin

Admin accounts cannot be created from the public mobile application. They must be provisioned via SQL:

-- 1. Create the user in Supabase Auth Dashboard (e.g. admin@milkdirect.com) and copy User UID
-- 2. Execute SQL to provision admin role:
INSERT INTO mk_profiles (id, name, phone, role, language, is_blocked)
VALUES (
  'PASTE_AUTH_USER_UID_HERE',
  'Master Administrator',
  'admin@milkdirect.com',
  'admin',
  'en',
  false
)
ON CONFLICT (id) DO UPDATE SET role = 'admin';

Step 3: Compilation & Execution Commands

1. Fetch Packages
flutter pub get
Resolves all dependencies including Supabase, Provider, and Material components.
2. Run Web Development
flutter run -d chrome
Launches real-time live reload on Google Chrome for rapid UI testing.
3. Build Android Release APK
flutter build apk --release
Compiles an optimized, standalone release APK ready for distribution.
